Axiom Research

A modular LLM-assisted research framework connecting scientific evidence, numerical modelling and experimental feedback through reusable Skills.以可复用 Skill 连接科学证据、数值建模与实验反馈的模块化 LLM 辅助研究框架。

Bring a research question, your available data and practical constraints. The host selects reviewed Skills, numerical tools calculate, and researchers decide how to validate the resulting analyses and proposals.提出研究问题，提供已有数据与实际约束。宿主选择已审核的 Skill，数值工具负责计算，研究者决定如何验证分析结果与建议。

Research workflow研究流程

An overall workflow, with module-specific support—not a claim that every module automatically completes the full loop.这是总体工作流，具体支持范围取决于模块，不代表每个模块都已自动完成整条链。 Check capability status查看能力状态

Who does what?谁负责什么？

Researcher研究者
Defines goals and constraints, approves proposals, performs external manufacturing and testing, and returns measured results.定义目标与约束，审核方案，在外部完成制造与测试，回传实测结果。
LLM hostLLM 宿主
Organises evidence and selects a suitable reviewed Skill at each technical stage—not just once per round.组织证据，并在每个技术阶段选择合适的已审核 Skill，而非每轮只选择一次。
Numerical programs数值程序
Execute reproducible calculations, diagnostics and comparisons within their documented scope.在已说明的范围内执行可复算的计算、诊断与比较。

When evidence or models are insufficient, request more evidence, calibrate or stop. Proposals are not automatically optimal or experimentally confirmed.证据或模型不足时，补证据、补标定或停止。提出建议不等于已得到最优方案或实验确认。
